FAQ

Does the app run on my computer?

Yes. Kalfi is a desktop Electron app. Shared hosting only serves this landing page. AI for Pro users goes through our VPS API; BYOK talks to your provider from the app.

Is my BYOK key uploaded to your servers?

No. Bring-your-own-key stays on device (encrypted). Only Pro hosted mode uses our server-side provider key.

Where do I manage subscription?

Subscribe from this site. Cancel or update payment in Stripe Customer Portal (linked after login). The desktop app only checks whether Pro is active.

Can it stay hidden on screen share?

Kalfi uses a panel-style overlay with content protection where the OS allows it — same category expectation as other stealth copilots.
