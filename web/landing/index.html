Questions

The things people ask before downloading.

Does my API key ever touch your server?

No. On the Free plan the key lives in macOS encrypted storage and talks straight to OpenAI or OpenRouter from your machine. Our API only appears when you choose Pro, and then the provider key is ours, not yours.

What actually runs where?

The app runs on your Mac — audio capture, transcript, session history, all local. This page is static hosting. The Pro backend runs on a small VPS and only handles login, subscription state and the model proxy.

Is this allowed in my interview or meeting?

That depends on the room. Kalfi is built for calls where assistance is permitted — client work, internal meetings, sales, practice, language support. If an employer, school or client says no, respect it.

Which providers and models work?

Anything OpenAI-compatible: OpenAI directly, OpenRouter for model choice, or a custom base URL for your own gateway. Chat and transcription models are configurable per install.

Windows and Linux?

The build targets exist, but macOS Apple Silicon is what ships tested today. The overlay and stealth behaviour are tuned for macOS first.
