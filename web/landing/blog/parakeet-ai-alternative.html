ParakeetAI Alternative: Same Live Answers, Fraction of the Price

ParakeetAI monthly list pricing sits around $149.90. The core job it does, which is to listen to a call, catch the question and put an answer on screen, is something Kalfi does on macOS for $14 a month on your own API key. Here is the full comparison, including where ParakeetAI is still the better buy.

14 September 2026 · 6 min read · Kalfi team

The short version

  • ParakeetAI list monthly: ~$149.90. Kalfi: $14 BYOK or $19 hosted.
  • Kalfi BYOK sends your calls to your own OpenAI or OpenRouter account at provider list rates.
  • Kalfi adds a custom app name and logo, so the process is not labelled with a product brand.
  • ParakeetAI has broader platform coverage; Kalfi ships tested on macOS, Apple Silicon.

What $149.90 a month buys

ParakeetAI is a capable live-answer tool with model selection and a credit-based path for lighter users. The subscription price reflects a hosted product: they run the transcription, they run the model calls, and they absorb the variability of heavy users. That is a legitimate business model. It is also why the number is large, because the plan has to cover the worst-case customer, not the average one.

The uncomfortable part is that for a typical user the gap between what the model actually costs and what the subscription charges is enormous. That gap is the entire argument for bringing your own key.

Running the numbers

Take a realistic month: twelve calls, forty minutes each, around 0.8 answered questions per minute. Each answer costs roughly 900 input tokens and 220 output tokens, plus Whisper-class transcription at about $0.006 per minute.

Transcription alone across 480 minutes is under $3. On a small, fast chat model the answer tokens add a low single-digit figure. Total provider spend for that month is commonly under $10, and often well under it. Add Kalfi's $14 and the all-in number lands in the low twenties.

Where BYOK is the wrong choice. If you do not want a provider account, a billing card on a third-party dashboard, or the small amount of setup a key requires, the honest answer is the hosted plan at $19/month. It is still an order of magnitude below $149.90, and there is nothing to configure.

The parts that are not about price

Answers sized for speaking, not reading

Kalfi leads with a single line you can say out loud, then supporting points below it. You pick brief, balanced or detailed, and formal, neutral or casual, before the call starts. When detection misses a question, Ask and Mic Ask force an answer on the last line rather than leaving you waiting.

Audio the way the call actually runs

System audio, your microphone, or both mixed together. That matters when you repeat a question back to the room, which is exactly the moment you want an answer forming.

Sessions that remember the project

Every meeting can generate a summary and sit inside a thread, so the follow-up call knows what was decided and what you promised. Browsing past meetings does not disturb the live session.

A name of your choosing

Hiding a Dock icon still leaves a product name in the macOS process list. Kalfi lets you set the display name and logo yourself. The stealth comparison goes into what that does and does not cover.

Stated plainly. Kalfi is not a rootkit and is not undetectable. Screen-capture protection depends on your macOS version and the conferencing app, so test your own setup before a call that matters, and follow the rules of the room you are in.

When ParakeetAI is the better choice

  • You are not on macOS. Kalfi's tested build is Apple Silicon, and the stealth behaviour is tuned there first.
  • You want a browser-based workflow with nothing installed.
  • You prefer a credit model over a subscription for very occasional use.
  • Your company already has it on an approved-vendor list and procurement is the real constraint.

If none of those apply, the price difference is difficult to justify, and the switch takes about ten minutes: download the DMG from GitHub Releases, paste a key or sign in, set your display name, and run one test call.

Questions before you switch

How much cheaper is Kalfi, really?

On list prices, roughly $149.90 a month against $14 BYOK or $19 hosted. Over a year that is about $1,799 versus $168 plus provider usage, or $228 flat on hosted.

What will my provider bill be on BYOK?

For twelve 40-minute calls a month it is usually single-digit dollars, dominated by transcription. Heavy daily use on a large model costs more. The landing page calculator estimates it from your own numbers using public token rates.

Do I lose model choice by switching?

No. Point Kalfi at OpenAI, at OpenRouter for a much larger catalogue, or at any OpenAI-compatible base URL. Chat and transcription models are configurable per install.

Is my key safe?

It is stored in macOS encrypted storage on your machine and used to call your provider directly. It never reaches our servers. On the hosted plan there is no key at all.

Is there a free plan?

No. The bring-your-own-key plan is $14/month for the app itself, so the desktop app has a funded maintenance path. A Team pack is in progress; email hello@chatlm.tech if you need seats now.

Pay for the model, not the middleman

$14 a month with your own key, $19 if you would rather skip the provider dashboard entirely.
